How to buy TCKL?

01
Install MetaMask

Download MetaMask or Trust Wallet for mobile or browser. Create a new wallet and save your seed phrase safely.

02
Add BSC Network

In MetaMask, add Binance Smart Chain:
RPC: bsc-dataseed1.binance.org
Chain ID: 56 | Symbol: BNB

03
Buy BNB

Buy BNB on Binance.com and transfer it to your BSC wallet. You need at least 0.01 BNB for gas fees.

04
Swap on PancakeSwap

Go to PancakeSwap, search for TCKL or paste the contract address. Set slippage to 5-6% and confirm!
